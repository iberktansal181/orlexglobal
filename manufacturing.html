Manufacturing & quality

Made on our own lines in Istanbul.

Efamed Medical has produced orthopaedic supports in Istanbul since 1993. Design, cutting, assembly, inspection and labelling happen under one roof — which is why we can change a size ladder, a packing unit or a label for you.

Process

From pattern to labelled pack.

Pattern & sizing

Anatomical patterns and full size ladders, from pediatric to XXL.

Materials

Neoprene, knitted elastics, breathable fabrics, thermoplastics, aluminium and steel stays.

Cutting

Material cut to pattern for each size and side.

Sewing & assembly

Seams, straps, stays and fastenings assembled on our lines.

Inspection

Finished products checked before packing, under our ISO 13485 system.

UDI labelling & packing

Each pack labelled with UDI-DI / GTIN and a QR code to its product page.

Quality system

Certified, documented, traceable.

  • ISO 13485 quality management for medical devices
  • ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 alongside it
  • CE marked range, Declaration of Conformity available on request
  • UDI-DI and GTIN assigned to every reference
  • Instructions for use printed in nine languages
